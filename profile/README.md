# Hi, I'm Vince

I work on Snowflake data platforms — pipelines, cost, access control, and the operational stuff that keeps them running. Lately that's included a fair amount of AI tooling on top of the data.

Everything here uses made-up names and data. The patterns are real; the employer and client details aren't.

## How I tend to work

- Figure out who's going to get paged before figuring out what to build.
- Write the runbook and the failure cases alongside the code, not after.
- Keep examples small enough that someone else can read the whole thing and run it.
- Say what I'd do differently. Most of the case studies have a section on that.
